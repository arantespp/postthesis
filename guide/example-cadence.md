# X growth playbook — cadence (example)

This file shows the shape of a guide. It is not the guide Postthesis runs on:
the validated rules are kept out of this repository, in the project's documents
under `/playbooks/x-growth/`, where the MCP server's `x-guide` tool reads them.

## Principles

- One rule per line, each with the reason it holds.

## Cadence by stage

| Stage | Article | Dense post | Short posts | Replies |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starting (< 1k followers) | 1 per week | 1 per day | 2 per day | 5 per day |
| Growing (1k–10k) | 1 per week | 1 per day | 2 per day | 5 per day |

## Fitting the time the person has

The hours the cadence above needs, and the order in which it is cut when the
person has fewer.
